My Learning Toolkit

As a Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student, I believe that continuous learning is important for academic success and professional development. This page shares study techniques and online resources that can support my learning journey.

1. Effective Study Techniques

Time Management

Creating a study timetable helps me organise my activities, set priorities and use my time wisely.

Active Recall

Testing myself without looking at my notes helps me remember information and identify areas that need more practice.

Practice Questions

Answering exercises and past questions helps me understand concepts and prepare for examinations.

Consistent Revision

Revising regularly helps me understand topics better instead of waiting until examination time.
