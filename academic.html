My Journey as a BIDA Student

Academic Year: 2026/2027

Introduction

Starting my journey as a Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student has been an important step towards achieving my academic and personal goals. As a first-year undergraduate student, I am exploring a new learning environment and developing knowledge that I hope to use in my future career. This journey is not only about obtaining a qualification but also about discovering my abilities and becoming a better version of myself.

Adjusting to Tertiary Education

One of the important parts of entering tertiary education is understanding that learning requires greater personal responsibility. I have to take an active role in my education, understand academic concepts and make an effort to keep up with my studies. Studying Business Intelligence and Data Analytics also exposes me to different areas, including business, mathematics, statistics and technology. These subjects require concentration, practice and a willingness to learn.

Time Management and Study Habits

Time management is an important skill that I need to continue developing. Balancing academic responsibilities and personal activities requires planning and discipline. I recognise the importance of setting study goals, organising my tasks and avoiding unnecessary distractions. I also understand that studying should not only involve reading information but also practising questions, reviewing difficult topics and identifying areas that need improvement.

Challenges and Personal Growth

My academic journey is an opportunity to recognise that learning takes time. Some concepts may require additional effort and repeated practice before I fully understand them. Instead of allowing difficulties to discourage me, I want to develop patience, consistency and a growth mindset. I believe that mistakes can become learning opportunities when I take time to understand and improve from them.

My Future Goals

My interest in business, finance, mathematics and technology motivates me to continue developing my abilities. I aspire to build a career in data analytics while also exploring entrepreneurship. My long-term goal is to achieve financial independence and create a future that makes me and my mother proud.

Conclusion

Overall, my academic journey is a continuous process of learning, adapting and growing. I understand that success requires commitment, discipline and the willingness to improve. As I progress through my studies, I aim to make the most of every learning opportunity and become a knowledgeable and capable professional.
