TEAMWORK AND COLLABORATION

My Group Work Experience

Understanding teamwork through reflection and personal development.

Introduction

Group work is an important part of academic development because it allows students to share ideas, communicate and work towards a common goal. Through group activities, students learn how to cooperate, manage responsibilities and appreciate different perspectives.

Tuckman's Five Stages of Group Development provide a framework for understanding how groups develop and work together.

01

Forming

Getting to Know the Group

The forming stage is when group members first come together. Members introduce themselves, become familiar with one another and begin understanding the task.

What I Learned From Group Work

Group work has the potential to develop important professional skills. It highlights the importance of communication, cooperation, accountability and respect for different opinions.

As I continue my academic journey, I aim to become a better team member by contributing positively, listening to others and taking responsibility for my work.
